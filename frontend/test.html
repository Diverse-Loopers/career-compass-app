Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2025

Diverse Loopers (“Diverse Loopers,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) is a business and skill-development ecosystem providing educational programs, career-pathway initiatives, technology solutions, and software development services.

We are committed to safeguarding the privacy, confidentiality, and security of personal information entrusted to us. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect personal information when individuals interact with our website, platforms, programs, or services.

By accessing or using our services, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary for legitimate academic, operational, and business purposes.

A. Information Provided Directly by You

  • Name and contact details (email address, phone number)
  • Institution / organization details (if applicable)
  • Educational background, academic records, and skills
  • Resume, portfolio, or professional profile
  • Date of birth (where required for eligibility or verification)
  • Location information (city, state, country)
  • User account credentials
  • Feedback, queries, and communications
  • Documents submitted for verification or assessments
  • Payment-related information processed through third-party payment gateways (We do not store full card or banking details.)

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • IP address
  • Device information (browser, operating system, device type)
  • Usage logs and activity records
  • Session data, clicks, navigation paths, and referral links
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies
  • Performance, diagnostics, and crash reports

C. Information Received from Third Parties

  • Authentication via Google, LinkedIn, or similar services
  • Partner institutions and collaborating organizations
  • Hiring and referral partners (where applicable)
  • Payment gateways and financial processors
  • Analytics, marketing, and communication service providers

We collect only information necessary and lawful for the purposes described in this policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

For Students / Participants

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Enroll in programs, courses, workshops, and events
  • Deliver personalized learning content and recommendations
  • Monitor performance, assessments, and project outcomes
  • Issue certificates, participation records, and reports
  • Provide mentorship and career-pathway support
  • Communicate updates, reminders, announcements, and notices

For Clients / Organizations

  • Understand project and service requirements
  • Deliver software development, consulting, and technology solutions
  • Manage contracts, billing, and customer support
  • Improve service quality, reliability, and performance

General Business Purposes

  • Maintain and improve website and platform functionality
  • Conduct internal research, analytics, and reporting
  • Ensure platform safety, security, and fraud prevention
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • Send newsletters, updates, and promotional communications (You may opt out at any time.)
3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Maintain secure sessions
  • Remember preferences

  • Enhance learning and user experience
  • Support limited marketing and remarketing activities

You may disable cookies via browser settings; however, some features may not function correctly.

We process information based on:

  • Performance of a contract

Consent may be withdrawn at any time.

5. Sharing of Information

We may share information only with trusted service providers and partners for legitimate purposes, including:

  • Learning, certification, and collaboration partners
  • Hiring and referral partners (with prior consent)
  • Payment gateways and financial processors
  • Cloud hosting and IT infrastructure providers
  • Analytics, communication, and support tools
  • Legal, audit, and regulatory authorities where required by law

All partners are bound by confidentiality and data-protection obligations. We do not sell personal information.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, including:

  • Secure, encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/SSL)
  • Restricted access control and authentication
  • Secure storage systems
  • Regular security monitoring and reviews
  • Confidentiality obligations for personnel

While we take reasonable protective measures, no system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide requested services
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Resolve disputes and enforce agreements

Data is securely deleted or anonymized when no longer required.

8. Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 16 years of age without verified parental or guardian consent. If such data is discovered, it will be removed promptly upon notification.
