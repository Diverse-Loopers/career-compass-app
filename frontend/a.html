What is the Path Analyzer?

“Which tech career is actually right for me — and what exactly should I learn next?”

Path Analyzer is an intelligent guidance system designed to bridge the gap between what students study in college and what companies actually expect. The tool evaluates your profile and generates a structured recommendation instead of generic advice.

Evaluation Core

  • Academic Background
  • Interests and Strengths
  • Current Skill Exposure
  • Future Career Goals

Who Should Use the Path Analyzer?

The Path Analyzer is designed for anyone confused between multiple tech fields or unsure about specialization.

Early Birds

1st and 2nd year students exploring technology clarity before specialization.

Job-Ready

Final-year students preparing for internships or placements seeking acceleration.

Graduates

Graduates unsure about the depth or relevance of their current skill specialization.

Switchers

Learners switching career domains looking for a structured, risk-free plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Path Analyzer a test?

No. It is a guided analysis tool that understands your strengths and industry opportunities to give you a clear growth plan.

Does it guarantee jobs?

No. It provides clarity and direction. Success depends on your consistency and effort in executing the suggested roadmap.

Does it replace counseling?

It enhances counseling with structured insights, and mentors can use the report to guide further.

Is it only for tech students?

Primarily designed for technology careers, but helpful for anyone exploring digital roles.
